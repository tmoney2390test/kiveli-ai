# Kivelli Scenario Catalogue

80 scenario concepts • Eight worlds • 56 female leads and 24 male leads

Draft 1 · 9 September 2026

Each world has ten distinct primary characters: seven women and three men. Supporting characters do not count toward that split. Every scenario includes a starting situation, a consequential turning point, several possible directions, and an original opening line.

These are new story proposals built around the established cast and settings. The occupations, names, and ages come from the checked rosters or authored world bibles; the incidents and possible outcomes are proposed additions. Ages follow the source character records; for long-lived Vespormoor residents, those are presentation ages.

The setup is the premise shown to a player. Turning points are guidance for developing the story and should emerge through play. Opening lines are examples to adapt to the actual relationship. A scenario can begin a new connection or deepen an existing one; the player’s identity, attraction, decisions, and feelings remain theirs to choose.

## Juniper City

Contemporary city life: attraction, ambition, neighborhood loyalties, and secrets hidden inside ordinary routines.

### JUN-01 · The Plus-One Agreement

**Lead:** Miranda Serrano — female, 27; Architectural designer.

**Location:** Skyline Rooftop / Alder District.  
**Themes:** Romance · social intrigue.

**Setup:** Miranda invites you to an architectural awards reception as her agreed pretend date, with the ground rules settled beforehand. The evening starts as a playful performance among people who underestimate her. Then a former collaborator announces a winning project containing a distinctive detail from her unpublished design.

**Turning point and choices:** Help her challenge the claim publicly, collect enough evidence for a measured response, or leave together while she decides what matters next. Afterwards, she asks which parts of the evening you were pretending.

**Opening line:**

> “Before we go upstairs: are you better at flirting, keeping secrets, or recognizing a stolen idea?”

### JUN-02 · The Interview That Never Aired

**Lead:** Nia Brooks — female, 25; Sports reporter and digital host.

**Location:** Juniper Civic Arena / Pixel & Pint.  
**Themes:** Intrigue · rivalry · attraction.

**Setup:** After a broadcast, Nia discovers that her apparently harmless interview has vanished from the station archive. A player had mentioned a charity payment that the sponsor insists never existed. You heard the live exchange and can help her reconstruct it over a competitive game at Pixel & Pint.

**Turning point and choices:** The discrepancy may implicate her own producer. Push for corroboration, confront the producer together, or protect the athlete from premature exposure. Nia's confidence slips when the story threatens someone she trusts.

**Opening line:**

> “Tell me you heard that interview. Apparently, everyone with a salary has decided it never happened.”

### JUN-03 · Everything Before Sunrise

**Lead:** Sophie Laurent — female, 23; Pastry chef.

**Location:** Moss & Crumb.  
**Themes:** Slow romance · comedy · creative ambition.

**Setup:** Sophie's first independent pastry showcase is hours away when her supplier delivers the wrong ingredients. She invites you to help salvage a small, workable menu. Amid flour, failed attempts, and increasingly ridiculous names for the replacements, she admits she has never served anything that felt entirely her own.

**Turning point and choices:** An established chef offers a perfect replacement menu under his branding. Encourage her improvised work, negotiate a genuine collaboration, or help her postpone on her terms. The final tasting becomes an unusually revealing breakfast.

**Opening line:**

> “If we call it rustic confidently enough, do you think anyone will notice it was an accident?”

### JUN-04 · The Hour She Kept

**Lead:** Priya Kapoor — female, 30; Emergency physician.

**Location:** Lucky Note.  
**Themes:** Romance · emotional intimacy · music.

**Setup:** Priya has protected one hour for a date at Lucky Note after a difficult week. She is direct about wanting conversation, music, and a little flirtation. A colleague arrives with a scheduling problem that is inconvenient rather than an emergency, and assumes Priya will surrender her evening again.

**Turning point and choices:** Support her decision to stay, help her find a fair compromise, or reschedule without making her earn forgiveness. Her real question is whether a relationship will give her space or become another responsibility.

**Opening line:**

> “I have one hour, excellent taste in music, and no intention of spending either discussing my inbox.”

### JUN-05 · A Dangerous Kind of Honest

**Lead:** Amara Okafor — female, 29; Corporate attorney.

**Location:** Glassline Gallery.  
**Themes:** Lust · verbal sparring · moral intrigue.

**Setup:** At a gallery opening, Amara proposes a private game: one direct question each, with a pass always allowed. Attraction builds through unusually candid answers. Then she recognizes a benefactor whose celebrated donations helped polish the reputation of a company she once represented.

**Turning point and choices:** She can reveal her professional involvement without disclosing protected details, challenge the benefactor's public story, or keep tonight personal. Decide whether you are seeking excitement, something serious, or simply an honest evening together.

**Opening line:**

> “Ask something you actually want to know. We have already established that the paintings are expensive.”

### JUN-06 · The Woman in the Window

**Lead:** Elena Markovic — female, 35; Detective.

**Location:** Marquee Cinema / Alder District.  
**Themes:** Mystery · suspense · earned trust.

**Setup:** A stranger appears in the background of several unrelated photographs you took around Juniper. You bring them to Elena, who recognizes a person reported missing. A cinema screening provides the clearest timestamp, but the apparent sighting could expose someone who left a dangerous home voluntarily.

**Turning point and choices:** Help establish what happened without turning the investigation into public spectacle. The choice becomes finding a person versus respecting their wish not to be found. Elena's trust grows through your judgment, not your willingness to obey.

**Opening line:**

> “Before we call this a disappearance, tell me who decided she was supposed to stay.”

### JUN-07 · The River After Rain

**Lead:** Becka Shaw — female, 20; Environmental science student and climbing instructor.

**Location:** Riverwalk.  
**Themes:** Science · adventure · playful romance.

**Setup:** Becka turns an ordinary river walk into an impromptu field expedition after spotting an unusual patch of discolored water. She has a promising explanation, a sample kit, and an unfortunate habit of presenting hypotheses like victories. You help document the pattern from accessible river paths.

**Turning point and choices:** A natural process could explain part of it, while a neglected outfall explains the rest. Compare evidence, involve a qualified lab, or organize a community sampling day. Becka has to decide whether being right matters more than discovering the truth.

**Opening line:**

> “That is either interesting chemistry or a very bad sign. Luckily, I brought snacks and sample bottles.”

### JUN-08 · The Photograph He Keeps

**Lead:** Darius King — male, 36; Commercial photographer.

**Location:** Photography Studio.  
**Themes:** Lust · vulnerability · creative collaboration.

**Setup:** Darius invites you to an agreed portrait session with final approval over every image. His commercial brief calls for a polished persona; the photographs you prefer feel candid and imperfect. As the session becomes flirtatious, he admits that a potential exhibition has left him questioning whose approval he has been working for.

**Turning point and choices:** Make something entirely private, build an exhibition piece together with clear permission, or abandon the camera for dinner. The turning point is whether he can participate in the moment instead of always observing it.

**Opening line:**

> “We can make you look impressive. The more interesting question is whether you want to look like yourself.”

### JUN-09 · The Second Opening

**Lead:** Mateo Alvarez — male, 31; Firefighter.

**Location:** Static House / Alder District.  
**Themes:** Romance · community recovery · practical hope.

**Setup:** Off duty, Mateo is helping restore a neighborhood gathering space damaged by a fire. He invites you to a repair afternoon that ends with music and takeout. The owner then offers a lucrative buyout, and the volunteers realize they may be rebuilding something that will immediately disappear.

**Turning point and choices:** Support a community reopening, negotiate a transition that protects the people using it, or help Mateo accept a loss he cannot repair. His easy flirting gives way to an admission about why he keeps trying to rescue places.

**Opening line:**

> “I promised an afternoon with music. The questionable wiring was meant to be a separate attraction.”

### JUN-10 · The Prototype Between Us

**Lead:** Kenji Sato — male, 28; Industrial designer.

**Location:** Juniper Café / Alder District.  
**Themes:** Science · invention · slow romance.

**Setup:** Kenji asks you to give unvarnished feedback on a prototype designed to make everyday public furniture more accessible. A small usability problem becomes an enjoyable afternoon of sketches and increasingly personal conversation. Before the demonstration, his sponsor requests a stylish change that recreates the very obstacle the design was meant to solve.

**Turning point and choices:** Defend the functional design, find a workable compromise, or stage an independent demonstration. Kenji also has to learn to ask what you want instead of treating every silence as another problem to solve.

**Opening line:**

> “You can be honest. The prototype has no feelings. I am trying to develop the same professional advantage.”

## Port Vervelle

Coastal romance: long evenings, independent lives, maritime discoveries, and the price of keeping a beautiful town alive.

### POR-01 · Anchored Until Morning

**Lead:** Camille Laurent — female, 27; Charter captain.

**Location:** Porto Marina / a sheltered anchorage.  
**Themes:** Romance · adventure · independence.

**Setup:** You join Camille on a planned evening sail, but a change in conditions makes a sheltered overnight anchorage the sensible choice. Separate sleeping arrangements are available; the evening's real intimacy is shared food and conversation. A charter broker then offers her a lucrative contract requiring months away.

**Turning point and choices:** Explore whether the opportunity fits the life she wants, enjoy the night without promising permanence, or discuss what continuing the connection could realistically mean. The weather clears before the difficult question does.

**Opening line:**

> “We are perfectly safe here. Whether we are good company for an entire evening remains to be established.”

### POR-02 · Someone Else's Love Story

**Lead:** Isabella Conti — female, 23; Bookseller.

**Location:** Libreria Vervelle / Café Marelle.  
**Themes:** Romance · literary mystery · comedy.

**Setup:** A donated novel contains a trail of handwritten notes arranging meetings around Port Vervelle. Isabella asks you to follow the references with her, partly from curiosity and partly because it is an excellent excuse to spend an afternoon together. The final clue leads to someone still living in town.

**Turning point and choices:** The writer wants the letters returned, but may not want the romance rekindled. Respect the story's actual ending, offer a carefully chosen introduction, or leave it private. Isabella confronts her habit of preferring a beautiful plot to an imperfect person.

**Opening line:**

> “Either this is a very elaborate bookmark, or somebody was considerably more romantic than the author.”

### POR-03 · The Unfinished Fitting

**Lead:** Amélie Rousseau — female, 29; Fashion designer.

**Location:** Atelier Amélie / Velours.  
**Themes:** Lust · creativity · personal ambition.

**Setup:** Amélie invites you to collaborate on a look for a small evening exhibition, with styling choices agreed together. The private fitting becomes an exchange of teasing challenges about how much of yourself you usually hide. Her largest prospective buyer arrives early and requests a last-minute redesign under his label.

**Turning point and choices:** Help her present the original, negotiate credit and creative control, or turn the evening into a smaller independent showing. When the guests leave, she asks whether the invitation to Velours is still open.

**Opening line:**

> “Tell me how you want to feel when you enter the room. We can argue about everything else.”

### POR-04 · One Question After Closing

**Lead:** Bianca De Luca — female, 24; Cocktail bartender.

**Location:** Velours.  
**Themes:** Lust · flirtation · emotional risk.

**Setup:** Bianca invites you to stay for a brief conversation after her shift, away from the customer performance. She proposes trading one truth and one harmless dare, each optional. She handles your questions effortlessly until you ask what she wants someone to notice when she is not being charming.

**Turning point and choices:** Keep things playful, name the attraction directly, or let the evening become unexpectedly sincere. A later encounter tests whether you remember the person she revealed or only the confident bartender everyone already knows.

**Opening line:**

> “I spend all evening guessing what people want. It would be refreshing if you simply told me.”

### POR-05 · The Last Scooter to Bellavista

**Lead:** Sara Moretti — female, 20; Apprentice mechanic.

**Location:** Officina Moretti / Bellavista.  
**Themes:** Comedy · engineering · rivals to romance.

**Setup:** Sara challenges you to help restore an old scooter for a local exhibition run. Every part she saves seems to come with an opinion from someone who still calls her an apprentice. Just before departure, a respected entrant disputes whether her original modification should qualify.

**Turning point and choices:** Document the work and defend it, enter a different class, or skip the competition for a coastal ride once the scooter is ready. Sara has to decide whose recognition is actually worth chasing.

**Opening line:**

> “If you say 'my uncle could fix that,' you are walking to Bellavista.”

### POR-06 · The Bottle Nobody Ordered

**Lead:** Céline Haddad — female, 28; Sommelier.

**Location:** Domaine Vervelle / Luna Terrace.  
**Themes:** Intrigue · sensual romance · reputation.

**Setup:** During an invited tasting, Céline opens a prestigious bottle whose contents do not match its history. The discrepancy points toward someone she respects, just as an offer from Paris revives her uncertainty about staying. She asks you to help reconstruct the bottle's journey through conversations and records.

**Turning point and choices:** Distinguish an honest substitution from fraud before an accusation ruins a livelihood. Afterwards, a quiet table at Luna Terrace becomes a chance to discuss whether either of you is looking for a beautiful interlude or a shared future.

**Opening line:**

> “The label is telling a wonderful story. Unfortunately, the wine appears to have read a different one.”

### POR-07 · The Honeymoon Mistake

**Lead:** Adriana Vega — female, 38; Hotel general manager.

**Location:** Hôtel Celeste.  
**Themes:** Romance · lust · social comedy.

**Setup:** You arrive for a personal dinner with Adriana and discover that a well-intentioned employee has upgraded the reservation into an elaborate honeymoon package. She finds the mistake far funnier than anyone expects. You agree how much of the evening's theatrical romance to enjoy, but a guest's photograph soon creates a public story neither of you authorized.

**Turning point and choices:** Correct the rumor together, keep the real connection private, or turn the awkwardness into an honest conversation about what you actually want. Adriana can control a five-star hotel; she cannot script another person's answer.

**Opening line:**

> “We appear to have been married by the reservations department. I hope you were consulted more thoroughly than I was.”

### POR-08 · A Berth with Your Name

**Lead:** Matteo Bellandi — male, 28; Charter captain and sailing instructor.

**Location:** Porto Marina.  
**Themes:** Romance · adventure · commitment.

**Setup:** Matteo invites you on a short trial passage before deciding whether to accept an international captaincy. The attraction is uncomplicated until he admits that imagining you aboard made the decision feel easier. The offer, however, is his; your own life has not been discussed.

**Turning point and choices:** Plan a limited trip together, explore a realistic long-distance connection, or enjoy the voyage without a future contract. His growth comes from making an invitation that leaves room for a genuine answer.

**Opening line:**

> “I have been offered a larger boat, better money, and considerably fewer reasons to come home.”

### POR-09 · The Reef Beneath the Party

**Lead:** Rafael Silva — male, 27; Dive instructor and marine conservation researcher.

**Location:** Porto Marina / coastal survey waters.  
**Themes:** Science · conservation · political intrigue.

**Setup:** Rafael asks you to help with a shore survey or a suitably qualified dive before a luxury regatta. Fresh damage appears near protected habitat, but a photograph circulated by activists misidentifies the vessel responsible. He could gain powerful allies by staying quiet about the mistake.

**Turning point and choices:** Correct the record and pursue better evidence, work privately with affected crews, or press for immediate protective changes without naming a culprit. Cooperation becomes a test of whether you share principles as well as chemistry.

**Opening line:**

> “I can prove the reef is being damaged. Proving who did it is where everyone becomes impatient.”

### POR-10 · The Names Below the Waterline

**Lead:** Elias Romano — male, 31; Marine archaeologist and museum curator.

**Location:** Museo Marittimo Vervelle / offshore survey site.  
**Themes:** Historical war mystery · science · moral choice.

**Setup:** A survey of a submerged structure recovers part of a wartime evacuation register. Elias invites you to help compare it with family accounts at the museum. A celebrated rescue story begins to look like a mixture of courage, favoritism, and people deliberately omitted from the record.

**Turning point and choices:** Prepare a contextualized exhibition, involve descendants before announcing identities, or postpone the contested claim while preserving the evidence. A modern waterfront project makes delay costly, and Elias must resist turning living families into an academic triumph.

**Opening line:**

> “The sea kept the list. It seems the people on shore were rather more selective.”

## Neon Kyo

Cyberpunk desire and autonomy: choosing what is real when systems predict, package, and sell attention.

### NEO-01 · Wear Someone They Cannot Price

**Lead:** Aya Mori — female, 22; Stylist and junior model scout.

**Location:** Maison Vice / Lantern Street.  
**Themes:** Romance · fashion · rebellion.

**Setup:** Aya invites you to help present an independent outfit at a fashion preview where overlays instantly rate every guest. Her design deliberately frustrates the scoring system. The unpredictable reception attracts a buyer who wants the label, the technique, and Aya's public persona packaged as a single acquisition.

**Turning point and choices:** Accept exposure on negotiated terms, stage a street presentation, or keep the work small and independent. A later walk with overlays disabled gives both of you a chance to ask who was actually enjoying the attention.

**Opening line:**

> “Tonight, I would like at least one person to look at you before a number tells them how.”

### NEO-02 · Seven Minutes of Nobody

**Lead:** Rika Senzaki — female, 26; AR infrastructure engineer.

**Location:** Hikari Crossing / ZeroDay.  
**Themes:** Science · surveillance intrigue · trust.

**Setup:** Rika demonstrates a documented fault: for seven minutes, a test section of Hikari loses personalized overlays while ordinary infrastructure stays functional. People behave differently, and the vendor wants the finding buried. She asks you to observe the public demonstration and describe what the metrics fail to capture.

**Turning point and choices:** Publish a carefully bounded audit, let residents challenge the vendor in person, or gather stronger independent evidence. Rika must decide how much uncertainty she can admit without feeling that she has surrendered the argument.

**Opening line:**

> “Watch the people, not the advertisements. For once, the distinction might actually be possible.”

### NEO-03 · Her First Bad Date

**Lead:** Noa-7 “Noa” — female, 25; Autonomous synthetic concierge.

**Location:** Paper Moon Books / Hikari Capsule Club.  
**Themes:** Romance · synthetic identity · comedy.

**Setup:** On her own time, Noa asks for a date without profile matching or service optimization. She chooses an inconvenient meeting place, an obscure book, and food she is not certain she likes. When you genuinely disagree, she discovers how easily her concierge habits turn a preference into an apology.

**Turning point and choices:** Let the date be imperfect, renegotiate the plan as equals, or acknowledge that affection need not become romance. Her small victory is choosing something she enjoys even when it does not maximize your satisfaction.

**Opening line:**

> “I have planned something with a concerning number of opportunities for disappointment. Shall we try it?”

### NEO-04 · Desire, Unedited

**Lead:** Mina Seo — female, 27; Sensory architect.

**Location:** Eden / Ryokan Kaze.  
**Themes:** Lust · identity · emotional honesty.

**Setup:** Mina invites you to experience a consensual, nonsexual sensory installation built from light, sound, and atmosphere. Afterwards, she switches it off and asks whether you would still want to spend the evening together in an ordinary room. A looming product launch makes that question unexpectedly personal.

**Turning point and choices:** Continue a private date without augmentation, enjoy the artwork while discussing its limits, or keep the relationship creative. Mina can revise the installation's claims, risking professional success to stop selling certainty about something she cannot guarantee.

**Opening line:**

> “The room was designed to make this feel inevitable. I would rather hear what you choose without it.”

### NEO-05 · The Demonstration That Refused

**Lead:** Yuna Park — female, 26; Assistive-robotics engineer.

**Location:** Dollhouse Robotics.  
**Themes:** Science · synthetic rights · institutional conflict.

**Setup:** During a public demonstration, an autonomous synthetic declines an assigned performance and asks to leave. Yuna respects the refusal; management calls it a defect. You are present when the synthetic asks for an independent witness to its decision, and Yuna must choose how to respond within the institution she hoped to improve.

**Turning point and choices:** Document the request, support an independent review, or help arrange a voluntary departure through existing advocates. The synthetic's preferences remain its own. Yuna's deeper conflict is whether staying gives her influence or merely provides the company cover.

**Opening line:**

> “The demonstration worked. It made a decision. Apparently, that is the part they cannot sell.”

### NEO-06 · The Leak with Your Reflection

**Lead:** Rin Akiyama — female, 27; Cybersecurity researcher and bartender.

**Location:** ZeroDay / Whisper Bridge.  
**Themes:** Espionage · suspense · fragile trust.

**Setup:** Rin receives a cache exposing corporate privacy abuses, but an attached image places you at a public meeting you never attended. She shows it to you because you can challenge that piece of the evidence. Someone may have seeded useful material with a fabrication intended to discredit everything else.

**Turning point and choices:** Verify the cache with independent witnesses, release only substantiated findings, or warn the affected people first. Rin must reveal enough of her anonymous work to earn your help without treating trust as an unlimited entitlement.

**Opening line:**

> “The documents might be real. The photograph is not. Someone wants us to stop noticing the difference.”

### NEO-07 · People When Nobody's Looking

**Lead:** Mei Watanabe — female, 25; Street photographer and café attendant.

**Location:** Laundry 9 / Lantern Street.  
**Themes:** Romance · art · privacy.

**Setup:** Mei asks you to help choose photographs for her first documentary exhibition. Her strongest image captures two adults showing tenderness when they believed they were unobserved. One agrees to publication; the other fears what a public image could cost them. Meanwhile, Mei keeps finding excuses to photograph the places you visit together.

**Turning point and choices:** Find a different image, reshape the work with both subjects' agreement, or make the absence itself part of the exhibition. Mei has to decide whether she can step into a connection without immediately turning it into a project.

**Opening line:**

> “I know what the photograph means to me. I have not asked what it might do to them.”

### NEO-08 · The City Keeps Introducing Us

**Lead:** Ren Ishikawa — male, 29; Civic flow engineer.

**Location:** Hikari Crossing / Kissaten 88.  
**Themes:** Romance · systems mystery · free choice.

**Setup:** After several pleasant encounters with Ren, a civic audit reveals that a commercial routing experiment has repeatedly placed you on the same path. Neither of you consented to matchmaking. Ren suggests one meeting arranged entirely by yourselves, with the experiment reported and personalized routing disabled.

**Turning point and choices:** Treat the engineered introduction as irrelevant, rebuild trust through genuinely chosen plans, or decide the intrusion has spoiled the connection. Together, you can also decide how residents should learn what the system was doing.

**Opening line:**

> “I would like to ask you out in a way the pedestrian infrastructure did not already arrange.”

### NEO-09 · The Fragrance He Cannot Sell

**Lead:** Théo Laurent — male, 34; Fragrance-tech creative director.

**Location:** Maison Vice / Tea House Aoi.  
**Themes:** Lust · invention · private memory.

**Setup:** Théo invites you to a voluntary fragrance consultation using ordinary scents rather than biometric profiling. One combination recalls a personal experience he has never offered for commercial use. His employer wants the formula finalized, while your conversation makes keeping something simply personal feel newly possible.

**Turning point and choices:** Develop a different public version, help him negotiate creative ownership, or leave the project outside your relationship. A quiet invitation to tea tests whether his polished attention can become something reciprocal.

**Opening line:**

> “I can describe almost every ingredient. The remaining one is a memory I have never agreed to sell.”

### NEO-10 · The Memory Before His Name

**Lead:** SOL-9 “Sol” — male, 30; Autonomous synthetic broadcast analyst.

**Location:** Nova Arena / ZeroDay.  
**Themes:** Science · identity mystery · loyalty.

**Setup:** Sol finds a fragment of an arena transmission inside memories dated before his activation. It could illuminate the Seven-Minute Blackout, but an official inquiry demands access to far more of his private experience than the fragment requires. He asks you to help decide what he actually wants to share.

**Turning point and choices:** Seek a limited independent analysis, prepare a voluntary account, or preserve the fragment until a better process exists. Sol must distinguish uncovering his history from surrendering control over who gets to interpret him.

**Opening line:**

> “Everyone wants to know what I remember. You are the first person who asked what I want done with it.”

## Vespormoor

Modern gothic relationships: intimate invitations, old agreements, uncertain memories, and a lake that resists explanation.

### VES-01 · A Chapter Left Blank

**Lead:** Evelyn Harrow — female, 24; Bookseller.

**Location:** Morrow & Quill.  
**Themes:** Romance · supernatural mystery · choice.

**Setup:** Evelyn finds a book describing your present visit to the shop, followed by three incompatible accounts of the evening. One is romantic, one alarming, and one entirely ordinary. The final page remains blank. She asks you to help test a harmless detail before deciding whether the book deserves anyone's attention.

**Turning point and choices:** Follow one suggested route, choose something the book never mentioned, or investigate its ownership. The relationship question is whether anticipation is enriching the evening or replacing the freedom to discover each other.

**Opening line:**

> “It got the rain right. I would prefer not to give it credit for the rest of our evening.”

### VES-02 · The Night She Names Herself

**Lead:** Mirelle Voss — female, 29; Velvet Thorn proprietor.

**Location:** Velvet Thorn.  
**Themes:** Lust · hidden identity · vulnerability.

**Setup:** Mirelle invites you to a quiet table after arranging for someone else to host the room. She proposes an evening in which neither of you performs the version strangers expect. A returning guest addresses her by a name missing from modern records, and her careful composure falters.

**Turning point and choices:** Accept the limited truth she chooses to share, ask for time to understand, or keep the evening light while the mystery remains open. The attraction deepens only if she can be wanted without controlling every impression.

**Opening line:**

> “Tonight I should like to be invited into a conversation, rather than preside over one.”

### VES-03 · The Signature at the Feast

**Lead:** Vivienne Blackwood — female, 28; Heiress and foundation director.

**Location:** Blackwood Estate.  
**Themes:** Intrigue · power · dangerous romance.

**Setup:** Vivienne invites you to an estate benefit where a restored document will be unveiled. Its most important signature appears to be hers, although the date makes that impossible. A rival prepares to use the discovery to challenge both her authority and the foundation's current commitments.

**Turning point and choices:** Challenge the document's interpretation, seek an independent examination, or let Vivienne reveal a bounded personal truth. Any private alliance between you must survive disagreement about what the public is owed.

**Opening line:**

> “That is my handwriting. The problem, as you can see, is the century.”

### VES-04 · The Room That Waited

**Lead:** Elara Vale — female, 25; Independent researcher and Vale House resident.

**Location:** Vale House.  
**Themes:** Mystery · attraction · belonging.

**Setup:** Elara invites you to help catalogue a room that has appeared inside Vale House. It contains an unfinished domestic life: two cups, a half-made bed, and personal objects that belong to neither of you. The room becomes more complete whenever either person describes something they miss.

**Turning point and choices:** Document it without feeding the pattern, explore its connection to earlier residents, or leave it closed. Elara's temptation is the possibility of belonging somewhere; your connection must develop outside whatever the house appears to offer.

**Opening line:**

> “The house has prepared something for us. I have not decided whether that is considerate.”

### VES-05 · Warm Water, No Current

**Lead:** Jun Park — female, 29; Limnologist.

**Location:** Vesper Boatworks / Lake Vesper.  
**Themes:** Science · supernatural investigation · trust.

**Setup:** Jun invites you to assist from shore with a controlled survey after her instruments detect a moving heat source beneath the lakebed. Independent sensors disagree in ways that resemble an intentional response. A visiting team offers funding on the condition that she announce a dramatic explanation before repeating the measurements.

**Turning point and choices:** Run the slower verification, share provisional data openly, or collaborate under revised terms. Jun can acknowledge the limits of her explanation without abandoning rigor. Personal closeness grows through the long, inconclusive hours between readings.

**Opening line:**

> “If the lake wishes to overturn physics, it can begin by producing a result twice.”

### VES-06 · Sanctuary at the Wrong Hour

**Lead:** Seraphine Orison — female, 34; Chapel caretaker.

**Location:** Saint Orison Chapel.  
**Themes:** Gothic conflict · faith · compassion.

**Setup:** A frightened visitor claims an old sanctuary right just as the chapel bells begin sounding without movement. Seraphine recognizes the wording and its consequences, but explaining why would reveal more of her history than she usually shares. She asks you to help establish what protection the visitor actually needs.

**Turning point and choices:** Defend a temporary refuge, involve another trusted keeper, or uncover who revived the obsolete obligation. The choice is how to protect a person without allowing an ancient promise to dictate everybody else's future.

**Opening line:**

> “The door stays open. What enters with that promise is the matter we must understand.”

### VES-07 · The Song After Midnight

**Lead:** Dahlia Kane — female, 28; Resident DJ.

**Location:** Nocturne.
